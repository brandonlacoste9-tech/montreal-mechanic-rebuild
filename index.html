Questions

Good to know

How does a mobile mechanic work?

Our van carries everything a shop bay has — jack, tools, diagnostic gear. We do the work in your driveway, office lot, or wherever your car is parked. You keep your keys and your day.

How much does it cost?

Services start at $139. You get a firm price on the phone before we come — no surprises, no upsells at your door.

Which areas do you serve?

All of Greater Montreal — downtown, West Island, Laval, South Shore and beyond. If your car is in Montreal, we come to it.

Can I really get same-day service?

Yes — call or book in the morning and we're usually at your car by the afternoon. Availability is first-come, so earlier is better.
